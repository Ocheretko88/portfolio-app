# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-07-17

## Status

Accepted

## Context

This is a portfolio meant to demonstrate engineering judgement, not just output.
A reader should be able to see *why* things are the way they are, and be able to
tell the reasoning was deliberate rather than incidental.

## Decision

We keep short Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), one per significant choice,
in `docs/adr`, following Michael Nygard's format. Each records the context, the
decision, and the trade-offs we accepted.

## Consequences

- Decisions are traceable and can be revisited with their original reasoning.
- Reviewers can evaluate judgement, not just the final diff.
- There is a small ongoing cost: a non-trivial choice should come with an ADR.
